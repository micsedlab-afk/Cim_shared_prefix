## R1/R3 — signed engine, source area, and S1 capacity

The submitted Eq. (5) area for a 128-token score engine is 128 unsigned 7-nm SRAM-CIM macros × 0.0032 mm² = **0.4096 mm²**. The adopted nonnegative signed-Q4×K4 mapping uses physically distinct K+ and K− storage (two banks) and four native unsigned operations, leading to **256 physical macros = 0.8192 mm² per engine**. This is a result for the implemented mapping, not a theorem excluding all alternative signed circuits. The published on-macro 4-bit ADC area is already included in the 0.0032-mm² source macro; external drivers/buses/reduction remain unknown.

| Incremental macro budget | Original 128-macro full engines | Corrected 256-macro signed engines |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 mm² | 2 | 1 |
| 2 mm² | 4 | 2 |
| 3 mm² | 7 | 3 |
| 4 mm² | 9 | 4 |
| 6 mm² | 14 | 7 |
| 8 mm² | 19 | 9 |
| 12 mm² | 29 | 14 |
| 16 mm² | 39 | 19 |

These corrected engine capacities devote all area to macros (zero external physical support). At 1 mm² with a hypothetical 0.5-mm² external-support reservation, zero full 256-macro engines fit; partial tiles may still be feasible.

The original S1 capacity was independently reconstructed: at 8 mm², 891 high-density 512×512 SRAM macros store **27.84375 MiB**, and 1464 dual-port 4-KiB macros store **5.71875 MiB**. The P2048 128-token-tile residency ratios are **445/512 = 86.914%** (S1-HD) and **91/512 = 17.773%** (S1-DP). These agree with the submitted paper's storage claim.

## R1 — placement and physical writes

At Llama-3.1-8B 32L/32Q/8KV/D128, P2048, B1, Aadd=8 mm², O128:

- Unique packed Q4/K4 key source: **32 MiB** for all layers.
- Physically signed K+/K− macro images: **64 MiB**. The maximum 8mm² CIM macro capacity at 0.0032mm² per macro and 512 B per macro is **2500 macros = 1,280,000 B = 1.22070 MiB**.
- Under the implemented fixed signed key mapping, at least **62.77930 MiB** of physical K bank images must be overwritten between successive decode steps if all 32 layers are computed. The optimistic first cold step requires 64 MiB of writes; average across 128 steps is **65,838,864 physical write bytes per batch decode step**.
- **Do not call these 64 MiB mandatory HBM bytes.** A GPU/source system may send a single packed 32-MiB K4 image and split it locally. Such splitting still requires implementation/area/time/energy evidence. The physical programming burden exists even with ideal original-key supply.
- The submitted model puts two d64 partial score combinations on the GPU. Hence at B1/P2048, **16 MiB per decode step** crosses the boundary if the two FP32 half-score streams are transferred. A locally merged completed-score stream would require **8 MiB** but must pay for its separate local digital merge. Streaming eight fully raw signed d64 partials would require **64 MiB** if no local signed combination is supplied.

At 8 mm² there is no complete 32-layer signed resident prefix image. The paper's statement that prefix placement is complete before timed decode requires explicit differentiation of persistent versus layer-streamed K, and cold/warm programming must be charged.

The submitted partial-energy numerator uses 128gB = **512B** native macro accesses per logical 128-token K tile. The accepted signed mapping requires four unsigned native operations per logical signed Q4×K4, or **2048B** native accesses. Preserve the paper's one-write-plus-one-read S1 denominator and its measured 5.835-pJ/native-CIM-access nominal input.

| S1 reference | Manuscript partial-energy crossing B | Recomputed signed crossing B | 7.8-pJ high-access case |

| S1-HD | 40.857 | **10.214** | 7.641 |

This conclusion is conditional on applying the manuscript's access-energy value to each native unsigned operation under compatible conditions. It is still only a partial **macro-energy ratio**.
