# OJCAS-2026-0120 — Reviewer-driven scientific redevelopment contract
**5 November 2026 · Source of truth: rejected eight-page OJCAS manuscript and reviews dated 3 November 2026**

## A. Fixed scope and nonnegotiable controls

The submitted paper "Where Is the Sweet Spot for SRAM-CIM in Shared-Prefix LLM Attention?" and its S0/S1-HD/S1-DP/S2 comparison are the research baseline. The existing experiments on `integration/final-framework` are evidence to scrutinize and integrate, not license to replace the thesis with a new attention-compression proposal.

- **Reference:** shared-prefix QK^T endpoint, 7-nm incremental macro area, A100 GPU. Preserve the original 728-point experiment (B=13 sizes, prefix=7 sizes, incremental macro area=8 sizes) as a **historical reproduction target**, not a qualified performance result. Original headline 65.9% favorable modeled cases / 1.97x stage speedup is on hold pending corrected signed-MAC mapping, fair GPU and area comparison.
- **S0:** paper's claimed 1248-TOPS INT4 A100 roofline is a *historical modeled input*, not a native LLMCompass or measured CUDA INT4 result. Recent native A100 80GB **FP16** LLMCompass code is a *different precision baseline*. Evaluate both separately; do not silently transfer timing or claim INT4-native execution.
- **S1-HD/S1-DP:** same shared-prefix K bit precision, occupancy and workload/score endpoint as S2, with measured published macro densities, explicit porting, programming/placement, cache/HBM paths and digital GPU score/remainder. S1 read/write energy versus S2 CIM compute energy is a **partial macro metric**; it is not a chip energy or total inference energy comparison.
- **S2:** retain published 7-nm unsigned Sinangil/Dong 64x64, 0.0032 mm2, 5.5 ns, O4 ADC macro properties. Physically account for signed K+/K- storage and **four unsigned native array evaluations for signed Q4 x K4**, output scaling/reduction, V (if used), buffering, row programming, control, interconnect and link. The published on-macro ADC is already included in the published complete macro; do not count it twice. Missing external/peripheral area is unknown or a named parameter, never physical-qualified "iso-area."
- **Primary model:** actual native Llama-3.1-8B, 32 Q heads, 8 KV heads, 128 dimensions, 32 layers, P=2048, suffix=128, B=1, O=128. Extend B, P, suffix and output length; for native Mistral preserve 4096 SWA and for OPT preserve 2048 context and MHA. Cross-model profiles and precision cannot be relabeled to fill missing experiments.
- **Numerical R0–R3:** original BF16 R0, post-RoPE Q4/K4 R1, O4 ADC mapped R2, characterized circuit R3 are preserved. Their absolute PPL + CIs must be reported. The newer SmolLM2-135M P128/P2048 centroid experiments are small-model **supplementary** evidence only; do not use them to justify all-layer Llama-8B compressed residence, O4 behavior or 7-nm speedup.
- **Novelty:** batching, prefix/suffix splitting, LSE merge, clustering and generic macro area/mapping optimization predate this work (Hydragen, Multipole Attention, CIM-Tuner, Ouroboros). A new placement policy, compressed representation or completed-prefix-attention path is a **candidate extension**. No novelty claim until a specific novel hardware dataflow / optimization is independently defined and wins direct ablations against prior alternatives.
- Preserve evidence labels: **measured GPU**, **real pretrained inference**, **source-executed operator model**, **published silicon macro**, **macro-only necessary floor**, **conditional sensitivity**, **physically qualified implementation**. Empty hardware metrics remain null. No additional foundry synthesis or invented MAC area.

## C. Required order, no shortcuts

1. **Baseline requalification first.** Recover precise paper S0 INT4 construction and S1/S2 original 728-point inputs, map them to corrected 7-nm signed execution, show diffs; preserve A100 FP16 separate, and require proper A100 query-batched shared-K GPU comparisons. Reject any unverified INT4 projection or fictitious measured CUDA timing.
2. **Placement and full-cost S1/S2 accounting.** Trace physical programming/dataflow and finite residency, correct the score endpoint, compare conventional S1-HD/S1-DP, digital SRAM+MAC threshold and signed CIM under the same area.
3. **End-to-end matched inference and quality.** Incorporate all decoder GPU operations and amortization over O/output and shared-prefix reuse; reuse 96 real Llama R0–R3 evaluations and expand genuine pretrained inference. Add optimized conventional GPU reference where execution is accessible, keeping source-only labels otherwise.
4. **Contribution gate.** Run ablations isolating new policy/dataflow from existing prefix batching, geometry, storage density and algorithmic compression. Require numerical and physical consistency. Reframe or stop positive accelerator claim if the benefit disappears.
5. Only then rewrite figures, methodology, related-work positioning and conclusions for a **new submission**, not a rebuttal that claims the rejection was a minor formatting issue.

## D. Baseline and claim acceptance gates

- **G0 Geometry/precision:** exact same B, P, suffix, O, GQA/MHA semantics, head shape, data precision, model revision, completed result endpoint. S0 original INT4 and A100 FP16 reported as distinct rows, never silently compared to O4-CIM.
- **G1 Physical:** complete macro counts, signed four-evaluation mapping, actual provisioning/readout paths, finite layers/ports and known external area. If external area unknown, say "macro-only" / "conditional" rather than "iso-area".
- **G2 Quality:** exact full pretrained checkpoint; R0–R3 and any centroid layer-selection path independently evaluated at *the same downstream endpoint*, with disjoint calibration/evaluation and reported absolute PPL.
- **G3 Runtime:** GPU full attention source measured or explicit source model, S1/S2 full same endpoint, no omitted suffix/FFN/projection; all placement/setup charged or amortized under a documented reuse count. Unknown GPU kernels remain null.
- **G4 Contribution:** falsifiable new mechanism and quantitative same-condition ablations against S0, both S1, digital+MAC threshold, fixed and optimized mapping policies. Distinguish prior art.
- **G5 Reporting:** reproduce or explicitly invalidate original numbers; complete ablation, breakdown and provenance for any positive claim; negative physical boundaries retained as rigorous findings.

No additional theoretical sweeps are a substitute for missing real measurements, complete processor operations or process-qualified physical rates.
