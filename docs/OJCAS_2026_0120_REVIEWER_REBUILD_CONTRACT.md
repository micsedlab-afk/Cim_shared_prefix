## A. Fixed scope and nonnegotiable controls

The submitted paper "Where Is the Sweet Spot for SRAM-CIM in Shared-Prefix LLM Attention?" and its S0/S1-HD/S1-DP/S2 comparison are the research baseline. The existing experiments on `integration/final-framework` are evidence to scrutinize and integrate, not license to replace the thesis with a new attention-compression proposal.

- **Reference:** shared-prefix QK^T endpoint, 7-nm incremental macro area, A100 GPU. Preserve the original 728-point experiment (13 batch sizes, 7 prefix lengths, 8 macro-area budgets) and its published **modeled QK-stage** results, including 65.9% coverage at 8/12 mm² and 1.97× maximum. The extension must quantify external signed processing, placement and full decoder scope separately, without changing original area/engine count or claims.
- **S0:** paper's claimed 1248-TOPS INT4 A100 roofline is a *historical modeled input*, not a native LLMCompass or measured CUDA INT4 result. Recent native A100 80GB **FP16** LLMCompass code is a *different precision baseline*. Evaluate both separately; do not silently transfer timing or claim INT4-native execution.
- **S1-HD/S1-DP:** same shared-prefix K bit precision, occupancy and workload/score endpoint as S2, with measured published macro densities, explicit porting, programming/placement, cache/HBM paths and digital GPU score/remainder. S1 read/write energy versus S2 CIM compute energy is a **partial macro metric**; it is not a chip energy or total inference energy comparison.
- **S2:** retain published 7-nm unsigned Sinangil/Dong 64x64, 0.0032 mm2, 5.5 ns, O4 ADC macro properties. Preserve the submitted **128-macro S2 engine with signedness handled outside its core**. Keep signed processing/reconstruction external to the submitted core as specified in the numerical method, without imposing an alternative signed-bank layout. Retain published on-macro ADC and macro area, and maintain the paper's **macro-area** scope.
- **Primary model:** actual native Llama-3.1-8B, 32 Q heads, 8 KV heads, 128 dimensions, 32 layers, P=2048, suffix=128, B=1, O=128. Extend B, P, suffix and output length; for native Mistral preserve 4096 SWA and for OPT preserve 2048 context and MHA. Cross-model profiles and precision cannot be relabeled to fill missing experiments.
- **Numerical R0–R3:** original BF16 R0, post-RoPE Q4/K4 R1, O4 ADC mapped R2, characterized circuit R3 are preserved. Their absolute PPL + CIs must be reported. The newer SmolLM2-135M P128/P2048 centroid experiments are small-model **supplementary** evidence only; do not use them to justify all-layer Llama-8B compressed residence, O4 behavior or 7-nm speedup.
- **Novelty:** batching, prefix/suffix splitting, LSE merge, clustering and generic macro area/mapping optimization predate this work (Hydragen, Multipole Attention, CIM-Tuner, Ouroboros). A new placement policy, compressed representation or completed-prefix-attention path is a **candidate extension**. No novelty claim until a specific novel hardware dataflow / optimization is independently defined and wins direct ablations against prior alternatives.
- Preserve evidence labels: **measured GPU**, **real pretrained inference**, **source-executed operator model**, **published silicon macro**, **macro-only necessary floor** and **conditional sensitivity**. Empty hardware metrics remain null.

## C. Required order, no shortcuts

1. **Baseline requalification first.** Preserve paper S0 INT4 construction, S1/S2 original 728-point inputs and 128-macro S2 geometry; recover upstream timing provenance where available. Quantify external signedness and support costs in a separately named extension; preserve A100 FP16 separately, and require proper A100 query-batched shared-K GPU comparisons. Reject any unverified INT4 projection or fictitious measured CUDA timing.
2. **Placement and S1/S2 accounting.** Trace modeled K4 supply, row programming, finite residency and the common score endpoint. Compare S1-HD, S1-DP and original S2 under the submitted macro-area budgets.
3. **End-to-end matched inference and quality.** Incorporate all decoder GPU operations and amortization over O/output and shared-prefix reuse; reuse 96 real Llama R0–R3 evaluations and expand genuine pretrained inference. Add optimized conventional GPU reference where execution is accessible, keeping source-only labels otherwise.
4. **Contribution gate.** Run ablations isolating new policy/dataflow from existing prefix batching, geometry, storage density and algorithmic compression. Require numerical and physical consistency. Reframe or stop positive accelerator claim if the benefit disappears.
5. Only then rewrite figures, methodology, related-work positioning and conclusions for a **new submission**, not a rebuttal that claims the rejection was a minor formatting issue.

- **G5 Reporting:** reproduce the original manuscript numbers as modeled, preserving their original scope; report any new physically integrated extension with separate assumptions and results; complete ablation, breakdown and provenance for any positive claim; negative physical boundaries retained as rigorous findings.

No additional theoretical sweeps are a substitute for missing real measurements, complete processor operations or process-qualified physical rates.
